## 1. One-line summary

<!-- This study in one sentence. e.g. A prompt is a request you make to the AI, and there are 5 ways to write good ones. -->

A prompt is a request you make to the AI, and to write good ones you give it a role, context, format, and examples, and split the work up.

## 2. Key points

<!-- 3 to 7, in your own words, not copied. Adding an example from your own work makes it easier to remember later.
     e.g. 1. Give it a role — tell the AI what role to play, like "You are a cafe marketer." -->

1. Give it a role — decide what kind of person the AI should answer as. e.g. "You are a cafe marketer"
2. Give it context — tell it who will read this and why you need it.
3. Set the format — say in advance what shape you want the answer in. e.g. as a table, in 3 lines
4. Show examples — show a model of the answer you want first. This is called few-shot.
5. Split it up — don't hand over everything at once; ask step by step.
