# How to Use the Templates

1. Copy the template you need and save it in `notes/` under a new name — the naming rule is in the comment at the top of each template.
2. Fill in every `{{ }}` spot. If there is nothing to write, don't delete the section; write `(not in notes)`.
3. Go through the checklist below from the top.

| Template | When | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| `minutes.md` | Right after a meeting | Decisions · Action items · To discuss next · Next meeting |
| `study-notes.md` | The day you take a lecture or read a book | One-line summary · Key points · New words · Watch out for · Questions to ask · Things to try · What happened when I tried |
| `weekly-review.md` | Friday | Went well · Fell short · Try next week (KPT) |

## Checklist

**All templates**
- [ ] Section names and order are the same as the template (no section deleted — if there is nothing to write, `(not in notes)`)
- [ ] No `{{` is left (search for `{{` in your editor)
- [ ] Dates have the day of the week, and the date in the file name matches the date in the title
- [ ] Numbers, names, and dates were checked once against the original memo (to avoid mistakes like the 6,000 won price error on 10/30)

**Meeting minutes**
- [ ] Each decision includes "what · how much/how · by when"
- [ ] Every row in the action items table has an owner and a due date (if blank, it wasn't decided in the meeting → move it to To discuss next)
- [ ] All action items in the original memo were moved into the table (number of "do ~" lines in the original = number of table rows)
- [ ] The next meeting date is written down

**Study notes**
- [ ] Key points are written in my own words, not copied, and there are 3 or more
- [ ] Every unfamiliar word is in the "New words" table with its meaning
- [ ] Things to try are actions with "when · what" ("write better" ✗ → "put role+format in Monday's Instagram post" ✓)

**Weekly review (KPT)**
- [ ] The body has only three sections — Went well / Fell short / Try next week — in this order
- [ ] All three sections have at least 1 item
- [ ] Went well and Fell short each have a "why"
- [ ] Try next week items are actions with dates, and at least one connects to something that fell short
