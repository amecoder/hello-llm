# organizer — Memo Organizing Rules

This folder takes the original memos in `inbox/`, organizes them with the templates in `templates/`, and saves them in `notes/`.

## Rules
1. **Never edit the original memos in `inbox/`.** Only read them, and write results as new files in `notes/`.
2. **Use the templates in `templates/` when organizing.** Don't change section names or order (how to use them and the checklist: `templates/README.md`).
3. **Write dates like `10/27(Mon)`.** Month/day + day of the week in parentheses.
4. **Don't make up anything that isn't in the memo.** Don't delete empty sections; write `(not in notes)`.
5. **Write action items as a table and always include the owner and due date.** If they aren't in the memo, fill in `(not in notes)`.

Example:

| Action item | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Make presentation slides | Minji | 10/30(Thu) |
