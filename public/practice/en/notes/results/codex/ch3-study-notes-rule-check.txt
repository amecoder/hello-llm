## 1. One-line summary

A prompt is a request you make to the AI, and you can make it more specific with five methods: give it a role, context, format, and examples, and split the work into separate requests.

## 2. Key points

1. Set a role — tell the AI from what position to answer. For example, ask "You are a cafe marketer."
2. Explain the context — say who will read it and why you need it.
3. Set the output format — specify the shape of the answer you want, like a table or a three-line summary.
4. Give examples — showing an example of the result you want is called few-shot.
5. Split the work — rather than handing over several tasks at once, ask for them separately.

## 4. Watch out for

- AI answers can be wrong even when they sound convincing, so always check numbers, names, and dates.
