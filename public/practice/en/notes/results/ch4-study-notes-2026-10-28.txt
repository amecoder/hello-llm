## 1. Key points

**A prompt is a request you make to the AI.** The 5 tips the instructor gave for writing good ones:

1. **Give it a role** — e.g. "You are a cafe marketer"
2. **Give it context** — who will read it, why you need it
3. **Set the format** — e.g. as a table, in 3 lines
4. **Show examples** — doing this is called few-shot
5. **Don't ask for everything at once; split it up**

**Caution:** AI can tell convincing lies (hallucinations). **Always check numbers, names, and dates yourself.**
