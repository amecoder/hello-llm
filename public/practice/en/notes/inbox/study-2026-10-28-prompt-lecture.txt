10/28 notes I took during an AI prompt lecture (1 hour)

prompt = a request you make to the AI
how to write good ones?? the instructor gave 5 tips
1 give it a role - like "you are a cafe marketer"
2 context - who will read it, why you need it
3 set the format - as a table, in 3 lines, etc.
4 show examples (called few-shot)
5 don't ask for everything at once, split it up

words I didn't know: token? -> small pieces that text is cut into. length limits are set by these
hallucination = a convincing lie. always check numbers names dates!!
