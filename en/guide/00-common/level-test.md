# AI Knowledge Level Test — From Beginner to Expert

> **One-line summary**: Test what you know about AI and LLMs across four levels: **Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Expert**. Each level has 50 questions across 5 subjects, and passing unlocks the next level.

## About the Test

- **It works like a written certification exam.** For each level, you answer **50 multiple-choice questions** (10 questions in each of 5 subjects), choosing from 4 options.
- **Passing score**: You need to get **at least 30 questions (60%)** right overall, and **at least 4 questions (40%)** right in **every subject**. If you get fewer than 4 right in even one subject, you do not pass—even if you get everything else right.
- **You move up one step at a time.** Only the Beginner level is open at first. Each time you pass, the next level unlocks.
- For each level, 10 questions per subject are drawn from a question bank, so the questions change slightly every time you retake it.
- After grading, you will see your score for each subject, explanations for the questions you missed, and links to **study materials**.

| Level | Who it is for | Where to study |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner | People who are new to AI chatbots or use them occasionally | Chapters 1–4 and [Chapter 9](09-safety.md) of this guide |
| Intermediate | People who often use AI for work and study | Chapters 4–11 of this guide |
| Advanced | People who want to understand how AI actually works under the hood | [Chapter 1](01-what-is-llm.md) and the official docs and papers linked in the explanations |
| Expert | People who build or run AI services | The papers, official docs, and security guidelines linked in the explanations |

> **The Advanced and Expert levels go beyond this guide.** They ask about deeper topics like tokens, training methods, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agents, and security.
> Every missed question includes a link to the source material, so you can study those resources and try again. Service-specific features are covered in Stage 2 ([ChatGPT](/en/guide/11-chatgpt/) · [Claude](/en/guide/10-claude/) · [Gemini](/en/guide/12-gemini/)), coding tools in Stage 3 ([Claude Code](/en/guide/20-claude-code/) · [Codex](/en/guide/21-codex/) · [Antigravity](/en/guide/22-antigravity/)), and the development process in [Stage 4 AI-SDLC](/en/guide/30-ai-sdlc/).

> **This badge is not an official certification.** It is a learning badge to check your own skills.
> Your answers and scores are calculated only inside this browser and are never sent anywhere. The levels you pass are saved along with your nickname only in this browser to unlock the next level. Clicking "Reset progress" on the start screen clears them as well.

If you just want a quick check on how well you understood Chapters 1–9, take the [Understanding Check Quiz](certificate.md) (20 questions) first.
