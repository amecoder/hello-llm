# Stage 1 · Universal Guide to All LLMs

Whether you use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, what you learn in this stage works the same everywhere.
We recommend reading the chapters in order. You can check what you read right away with the **Quick Quiz** at the end of each section.

| Chapter | Title | What You Will Learn |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [What Is an LLM?](01-what-is-llm.md) | How AI creates answers, and why it sometimes gets things wrong |
| 2 | [Why All the Buzz?](02-why-it-matters.md) | What changed to make everyone talk about it |
| 3 | [Why You Should Learn It](03-why-learn.md) | The difference between people who use it well and those who don't |
| 4 | [Prompt Basics](04-prompt-basics.md) | How to write requests that get good answers |
| 5 | [Prompt Patterns](05-prompt-patterns.md) | Eight request tips and key terms, including Few-shot and CoT |
| 6 | [Refining Through Conversation](06-conversation.md) | How to ask for revisions well, and when to start a new chat |
| 7 | [Structured Prompts](07-structured-prompts.md) | Organizing long requests with Markdown and XML, and building your own in the prompt editor |
| 8 | [Meta-Prompting](08-meta-prompting.md) | Getting AI to write and improve your prompts |
| 9 | [Using AI Safely](09-safety.md) | Checking hallucinations, hiding personal info, copyright, and blocking hidden instructions (prompt injection) |
| 10 | [Hands-On Exercises](10-practice.md) | Nine exercises and a checklist to apply what you learned to your own work |
| 11 | [Using AI Like an Expert](11-expert.md) | How to decide what to hand off, test before handing off, and save repeated tasks |

[Ongoing Project — Preparing a Book Club](practice-kit.md) is a hands-on exercise where you carry one project from Chapter 11 through the end of Stage 2. You will download and use sample files.

The prompts from each chapter are collected by use case in the [Ready-to-Use Prompt Library](prompt-library.md). Copy them and just fill in the blanks.

Once you finish reading, take the 20-question [Understanding Check](certificate.md) to see your AI understanding level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).

If you want a deeper test, challenge yourself with the four levels—Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Expert—in the [AI Knowledge Level Test](level-test.md). Each level has 5 subjects and 50 questions, and passing one unlocks the next.
