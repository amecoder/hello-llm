# Ongoing Project — Preparing a Neighborhood Book Club Meeting

> **One-line summary**: If you practice something different in every chapter, you have nothing left in your hands at the end. Follow this **one** project from Stage 1 Chapter 11 to the last chapter of Stage 2, and you will end up with a **notice package** and **one custom instruction of your own**.
> All sample files were made up for this guide. They have nothing to do with real people or places.

## The Scenario

You are the coordinator for "Book Visit (책마실)," a neighborhood book club. The club has 20 members and meets once a month. You finished the October meeting last week, and now you need to prepare for the November meeting.

Here is what the coordinator needs to do:

- Organize the decisions and action items from the last meeting
- Write the November meeting notice
- Clean up the dues ledger and calculate the totals
- Check the meeting venue

You will do this work with AI, a little at a time across each chapter. If you have real work of your own, you can swap this out for that instead. See how to choose a task in [Chapter 11, "Choosing Your First Task to Practice"](11-expert.md#picking-your-first-task-to-practice).

## What You Will Have at the End

When you finish the project, you will have these four things in your hands:

1. **November meeting notice**: A complete post with the date, venue, book, dues, and discussion leader
2. **Dues summary table**: Income, expenses, and remaining balance, with duplicates and blank amounts fixed
3. **Action item list**: Who does what, and by when
4. **One custom instruction of your own**: A notice rule you can reuse next month. Example: "Keep notices under three paragraphs, and put the date, venue, and what to bring at the very top"

**Check Your Work**

- ☐ The date in the notice matches the "third Saturday" agreed on in the minutes
- ☐ If the venue is not confirmed yet, the notice says so
- ☐ You checked the totals in the dues summary table against the ledger yourself, and counted the duplicate row only once
- ☐ The action item list includes all three tasks from the minutes
- ☐ You saved your one-line rule in your custom instructions or project instructions

Once everything is checked off, try following the same steps next month with your own real work.
