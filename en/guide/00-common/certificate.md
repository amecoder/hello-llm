# Check Your Understanding — What Is Your AI Skill Level?

> **One-line summary**: Take a 20-question test to see how well you can use AI after reading Chapters 1 to 9. Depending on your score, you will get a **Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced** level badge.

## About the Test

- **There are two types of questions.**
  - **10 Concept Questions**: These ask about meanings and how things work, like "Why does AI sometimes make up book titles that do not exist?"
  - **10 Situational Questions**: These show you a real situation and ask what you would do, like "You asked this and got this answer. What should you send next?"
- Each chapter has at least one question of each type. These questions are only in this test and are different from the Check Quiz at the end of each chapter. The order of questions and choices changes every time you take the test.
- **Here is how your level is decided.**

| Level | Requirement | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner | 12 or more out of 20 (60%) | You can follow what you learned and ask AI to do tasks |
| Intermediate | 15 or more out of 20 (75%) + 7 or more situational questions | You can fix your prompts and check answers on your own, even in new situations |
| Advanced | 18 or more out of 20 (90%) + 9 or more situational questions + **Hands-On Task Self-Check** | You can give real work to AI, verify the results, and save your methods to reuse next time |

- If your score meets the Advanced requirement, a **hands-on task** will appear. You will give a real task to an AI in a chat window and check off 6 items on a checklist yourself. It is the same checklist as the final project in [Chapter 10: Hands-On Practice](10-practice.md), so if you do [Chapter 10](10-practice.md) first, you can check them off right away. If you are not doing the task right now, save your Intermediate badge first. Your score is not saved, so once you finish the task, you can take the test again to get the Advanced badge.
- You must answer all 20 questions before you can grade the test. Once graded, you will see the right answers, explanations, and which chapters to re-read for the questions you missed.
- You can put your name on your level badge and save it as an **image file (PNG)**. Feel free to share it on social media or messaging apps.

> **This badge is not an official certificate.** It is a learning badge to check your own understanding. The hands-on task is also a self-check, so no one else reviews it for you.
> Your answers and score are calculated only inside this browser and are never saved or sent anywhere.
> If you set a nickname on the first screen, it will be filled in on your badge automatically (your nickname is also saved only in this browser).

## If Your Score Was Low

Re-read the chapters for the questions you missed, and try the Check Quiz and Try It Yourself in those chapters first.
Above all, be sure to remember these three habits—they are the most important ones in this entire guide.

1. **Tell AI about your situation that it does not know** ([Chapter 4](04-prompt-basics.md))
2. **If the answer falls short, follow up and ask AI to fix it** ([Chapter 6](06-conversation.md))
3. **Check numbers, dates, and sources yourself in the original material** ([Chapter 1](01-what-is-llm.md), [Chapter 9](09-safety.md))
