# Chapter 9. Talking by Voice — Dictation and Voice Mode

> **One-line summary**: On the right side of the message box, there are two buttons for speaking. The **microphone** turns your words into text (Dictation),
> and the **blue wave button** lets you talk back and forth with ChatGPT like a phone call (Voice). Your voice can be saved just like chat history, so choose your location and topics carefully.

The screens shown here are from the web version (Plus plan) as of October 2026. Menu locations may look slightly different on the free plan or mobile app.

## The Difference Between the Two Buttons — Dictation and Voice

![The ChatGPT home screen. At the right end of the center message box "Ask ChatGPT (ChatGPT에게 물어보세요)", a gray microphone button and a blue circle button with a wave icon sit side by side. At the bottom of the screen, a "New Korean voices (새로운 한국어 음성)" notice and a "Try new voices (새 음성 사용해 보기)" button are visible](../../../guide/11-chatgpt/assets/shot-01-home-voice.png)

Chat titles and the account name in this screenshot have been replaced with examples. ① The microphone is Dictation, ② the blue wave button is Voice, and ③ the bottom banner is a notice about new Korean voices.

| | ① Microphone — Dictation | ② Blue wave button — Voice |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Turns your speech **into text in the message box** | Lets you **talk back and forth** with ChatGPT. Replies also come back as voice |
| Before sending | You **read and edit** the transcribed text before sending it | The conversation continues right away with no pause to edit |
| Best for | When typing a long question feels tiring, or when exact wording matters | When thinking out loud, when your hands are busy, or for speaking practice |

How to choose is simple. When you want a back-and-forth conversation or want to talk through an idea, use **Voice**. When you want to check and edit your spoken words as text before sending, use **Dictation**.[^voice]

Try Dictation like this. Press the microphone and say the lines below. Then check the text in the message box, fix any typos, and send it.

```
Recommend three day-trip spots near Seoul to take my parents next Saturday.
Pick places that don't require much walking.
```

Names of people or places are easy to transcribe wrong. Reading the text once before sending is the big advantage of Dictation.

## Trying a Voice Conversation

1. Press the **blue wave button** on the right side of the message box. In the mobile app, it is the voice icon on the message bar.[^voice]
2. The first time you use it, allow your browser to use the microphone when asked. You need to allow this for your voice to go through.
3. Speak just as you normally would. When you are done, press the end button on the screen to finish the conversation.

<!-- Screenshot needed: Web screen showing an active voice conversation after pressing the blue wave button (including the end button location) -->

You can only have **one voice conversation at a time**.[^voice] When speaking, the same tips you learned in Stage 1 still apply. Say who it is for and what format you want the answer in ([Stage 1, Chapter 4](../00-common/04-prompt-basics.md)).

Here are two good requests to try out loud.

```
I have a job interview next week. Act as the interviewer and ask me one question at a time.
After I answer, briefly tell me one thing I did well and one thing to improve, then move on to the next question.
```

```
I want to practice speaking English. Have a short conversation with me where I'm ordering at a cafe.
If my pronunciation or phrasing sounds awkward, briefly let me know in Korean.
```

::: details What the reply looks like (example — click to expand)
This is an example created for this guide. Actual replies vary depending on the service and the moment.

> Hi! Welcome. What can I get for you today?

If you say, "I want one coffee," it continues like this:

> Sure! What size would you like?
> "I want" works too, but saying "Can I get a coffee?" sounds more natural.

**Check if you got a good result**: Does it keep the exchange short, just a sentence or two at a time? Does it briefly point out awkward phrasing? If the explanation gets too long, say "Keep it shorter."

:::

If the answer is too long, just say "Keep it shorter" out loud. It works the same way as asking for changes in a text chat ([Stage 1, Chapter 6](../00-common/06-conversation.md)).

While driving, you can also talk with ChatGPT by voice through **CarPlay** (Apple's feature that connects your iPhone to your car screen). It works on all plans, worldwide. You need an iPhone running iOS 26.4 or later. However, **it cannot see maps, vehicle information, or your current location.** So you cannot use it in place of a navigation app.[^carplay]

## Voice Modes and Voice Styles

### Three Modes

There are three voice conversation modes.[^voice]

- **Live**: Listens and speaks at the same time. This means you can interrupt while ChatGPT is talking. Video and screen sharing are not supported.
- **Advanced**: The earlier real-time mode. In the mobile app, you can talk while showing your camera feed or screen.
- **Standard**: Transcribes your speech into text first, then replies. You take turns one at a time.

If you need video or screen sharing, use Advanced mode in the mobile app. For example, you can point your camera at a broken appliance and ask, "What does this blinking light mean?"

### Choosing a Mode and Voice

On the web, click your account name → Settings (설정) → **Voice style (음성 스타일)** to choose a voice and language. When opened on the web (Plus) screen on October 23, 2026, Voice style had the following items.

![The "Voice style (음성 스타일)" screen in Settings. The red numbers in the image match the numbers in the table below.

| No. | Item | Description on screen |
|---|---|---|
| ① | Voice style (음성 스타일) | Choose the voice used for voice chat and Read Aloud |
| ② | Language (언어) | The language to speak in (for example, Korean) |

## How Much Can You Use It?

Live mode in voice conversations counts your limit based on **how much time you have used in the last 24 hours**. ChatGPT lets you know when you reach the limit.[^voice]

| Plan | Usage per 24 hours (checked 2026-10-23) |
|---|---|
| Free | Limited access |
| Go | 3 hours |
| Plus | 3 hours |
| Pro | 15 hours depending on tier, or unlimited subject to abuse guardrails |

> These numbers change often. Check the help center link in the footnote for the latest figures.

## Voice History and Privacy

### Your Voice Is Saved Too

How long recordings are kept depends on the mode.

- **Live and Advanced**: Audio clips are kept with your chat history for 30 days.
- **Standard**: After your speech is transcribed into text, the recording is deleted.
- **Dictation (microphone)**: The recording is kept for as long as that chat stays in your history. When you delete the chat, the recording is deleted along with it within 30 days. However, there are exceptions, such as for security or legal reasons, or if you agreed to share audio for training.[^dictation]

The recordings themselves are not used for training unless you separately allow sharing. You must turn on "Include your audio recordings" (내 오디오 녹음 포함) for recordings to be used in training. However, the **transcribed text** follows your "Improve the model for everyone" (모델 개선을 위한 데이터 학습 허용) setting, just like regular text chats.[^voice] We covered this setting in [Chapter 8](08-privacy.md).

### Be Careful in Public Places

Spoken conversations **can be heard by people around you.** Not only your questions, but ChatGPT's replies also play out loud through your speaker.

- On the subway, in a cafe, or in an office, wear earphones or use **Dictation** to enter your words quietly instead of having a voice conversation.
- **Do not say out loud any information you should not type**, such as names, phone numbers, addresses, or medical records, as covered in [Stage 1, Chapter 9](../00-common/09-safety.md#what-not-to-put-in). When speaking, it is easier to let things slip without thinking than when typing.
- When other people are around, make sure their voices or conversations do not get recorded along with yours.
- Connected apps ([Chapter 7](07-projects-plugins.md#setting-permissions-for-connected-apps)) may not work in voice conversations. If one does not work, try typing the same request instead.[^apps-voice]

**Before having ChatGPT transcribe a meeting, tell everyone attending and get their consent first.** The **Meetings plugin** in the Mac desktop app (Pro and Business plans, beta) transcribes meetings and creates summaries and action items. The consent reminder shown by the app is only visible to you—it is not sent to other attendees. Once the notes are finished, the audio recording is deleted and cannot be played back.[^meetings]

## Try It Yourself

**1. Same question, two ways**

Try asking the question below once using the **microphone (Dictation)**, and once in a **new chat using the blue wave button (Voice)**.

```
I have eggs, tofu, and green onions in the fridge. Tell me two side dishes I can make in 15 minutes.
```

Then ask yourself two questions. Does the text placed in the message box by Dictation match what you said? In Voice mode, which feels more comfortable—listening to the answer or reading it?

**2. Try interrupting**

In a voice conversation, ask for a long explanation, then say "Hold on, make it shorter" while ChatGPT is still talking. Since Live mode listens even while speaking, check whether it picks up your interruption and adjusts.

**Ongoing project** ([Preparing a Book Club](../00-common/practice-kit.md)): In a voice conversation, say, "Help me practice a 30-second opening greeting for my book club." Deliver your greeting out loud and ask ChatGPT to point out one thing to improve.

## Summary

- The microphone is **Dictation**: it turns your speech into text in the message box, and you check it before sending.
- The blue wave button is **Voice**: you talk back and forth out loud. You can only have one voice conversation at a time.
- There are three modes: Live, Advanced, and Standard. On the web, you pick a voice and language under Settings (설정) → Voice style (음성 스타일) (the option for Live, Advanced, or Standard may not appear depending on your screen). Video and screen sharing only work in Advanced mode on the mobile app.
- Usage limits are counted over a rolling 24-hour period, vary by plan, and change often.
- Recordings in Live and Advanced modes are kept for 30 days, and Dictation recordings are kept as long as the chat remains in your history. Before transcribing a meeting, get everyone's consent first. Transcribed text follows your training settings. In public places, use Dictation or earphones, and do not speak personal information out loud.

## Key Terms

| Term | One-line meaning |
|---|---|
| Dictation | A feature that turns your spoken words into text in the message box |
| Voice | A feature for talking back and forth with ChatGPT out loud |
| Live | A voice mode that listens and speaks at the same time |
| Advanced | A voice mode that supports video and screen sharing |
| Standard | A voice mode that transcribes your words first and replies in turns |
| Transcript | A written record of what was said |
| CarPlay | An Apple feature that connects your iPhone to your car screen |
