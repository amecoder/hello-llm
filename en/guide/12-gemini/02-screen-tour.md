# Chapter 2. Tour the Screen — Where Everything Is

> **One-line summary**: On the Gemini screen, you only need to know four places: the **left sidebar** (past chats), the **center text box** (where you ask questions, plus "+"),
> the **model button on the right of the text box** (model and thinking level), and **Settings (설정) at the bottom left**. At first, using just the text box is enough.

This screen is from the web app in October 2026, and the screenshots show the Korean interface on a paid plan (Google AI Pro). On a free account, some menus may not appear or may have a lock icon. If a name on your screen differs, look for a button with a similar name.

When you sign in, you see the greeting "How can I help you? (무엇을 도와드릴까요?)" and the text box in the center.[^screen]

## The Sidebar — New Chats and Past Conversations

From top to bottom, the left sidebar has the following items.[^screen]

- **Search for chats (채팅 검색)** (magnifying glass): Find past conversations by keyword.
- **New chat (새 채팅)**: Open a new conversation. Click here when you change topics ([Chapter 1](01-getting-started.md)).[^start]
- **Library (라이브러리) · Images (이미지)**: View the files and pictures you uploaded or created in one place ([Chapter 4](04-files-create.md)).
- **Scheduled (예약됨)**: View tasks set to run automatically at a chosen time.
- **Customize (맞춤설정)**: Add skills and connected apps.
- **Notebooks (노트북)**: Group materials and chats by topic. Includes New notebook (새 노트북) and All notebooks (모든 노트북).
- **All (전체)**: The list of conversations you have had so far. The most recent chat is at the top.

At the very bottom, you will find your account name, your plan (for example, "Pro"), and the gear icon for Settings (설정).[^screen]

You can fold and unfold the sidebar with Close sidebar (사이드바 닫기) (the square icon) at the top left.[^screen]

When showing your screen to others, fold the sidebar first. Even the titles in your chat list can reveal what you have been asking.

It looks like a lot of menus, but at first you only need to know Upload files (파일 업로드). We cover the rest one by one starting in Chapter 4.

You can upload **up to 10 files** at a time. Each video can be up to 2 GB, and any other file can be up to 100 MB.[^files]

Click the microphone (Voice input (음성기록)) on the right side of the text box to speak your prompt instead of typing.[^screen]

### Editing a Sent Question

If you want to fix a question you already sent, click Edit text (텍스트 수정) (the pencil icon) next to your question. After editing, click Update (업데이트) and Gemini will generate a new answer.[^start]
This is useful when you realize later that you left out a condition. You do not have to retype the whole question from scratch.

For example, if you sent the prompt below and forgot to mention "to a coworker," edit the question and get a new answer.

```
Write a short message asking to move next Tuesday's meeting to Thursday.
```

## The Model Button — Model and Thinking Level

Click the model button on the right side of the text box (for example, "Argon High (Argon 높음)") to choose the **model** and **thinking level**.[^screen]
The model is the type of AI that creates the answer, and the thinking level is how long it thinks things through before answering.

![Menu opened by clicking the model button. Red numbers mark ① the model button "Argon High (Argon 높음)", ② the model list "3.5 Flash-Lite — Fastest answers (가장 빠른 답변 제공)", "3.8 Flash — Get help with anything (무엇이든 도움을 받으세요)", "3.1 Pro — Advanced reasoning (고급 추론)", "4 Argon — Complex tasks (복잡한 작업)" (checked), ③ "Thinking level (사고 수준)", and ④ the expanded thinking levels "Low — Fast and efficient (낮음 — 빠르고 효율적)", "Medium — Balanced depth (보통 — 균형 잡힌 깊이)", and "High — Very thorough (높음 — 매우 철저함)" (checked)](../../../guide/12-gemini/assets/shot-04-model-marked.png)

This is the Google AI Pro screen. Clicking ① the model button opens ② the model list, and hovering over ③ Thinking level (사고 수준) expands ④ the three levels. The checkmark shows what is currently selected.[^screen]

### Choosing a Model

| Model | Screen Description | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Flash-Lite | Fastest answers (가장 빠른 답변 제공) | Short, easy questions |
| Flash | Get help with anything (무엇이든 도움을 받으세요) | Most everyday tasks |
| Pro | Advanced reasoning (고급 추론) | Complex questions, hard coding |
| Argon | Complex tasks (복잡한 작업) | Very hard and complex work |

Source: Gemini web screen and Google Help[^screen][^models]

The number in front of a model name (such as 3.8 Flash) is the version number. Version numbers change often, so remember each model by its **role** rather than the number.[^models]

**Free accounts cannot choose a model manually.** Gemini automatically picks a model that fits your question, or answers with Flash-Lite.[^models]
You need a paid plan (a Google AI plan) to switch models yourself. A lock icon on a model means you need to upgrade your plan to use it.[^models]

### Thinking Level — How Deeply Should It Think?

Thinking level (사고 수준 ▸) has three steps.[^screen][^models]

| Thinking Level | Screen Description | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Low (낮음) | Fast and efficient (빠르고 효율적) | Simple questions |
| Medium (보통) | Balanced depth (균형 잡힌 깊이) | Tasks that need some thought |
| High (높음) | Very thorough (매우 철저함) | Hard tasks that need multiple steps |

Source: Gemini web screen and Google Help[^screen][^models]

If you do not choose one, the default is Low (낮음).[^models] Some Korean help pages call the middle level "중간," but the label on the Korean screen is "보통."[^screen]

### When to Turn It Up

**More advanced models and higher thinking levels use more of your usage limit.**[^models] So you do not need to keep them at the highest setting all the time.
Normally, use the default (Low) or a lighter setting, and move up one step only when an answer feels shallow or wrong. "Argon High (Argon 높음)" in the screenshot was chosen just to show the menu in this guide. Argon only appears on Pro and Ultra plans ([Chapter 1](01-getting-started.md)). Making the AI think longer does not always produce a better answer either ([Stage 1 Chapter 5](../00-common/05-prompt-patterns.md)).

Keep two things in mind.[^models]

- **Once you pick a model, it stays in that conversation.** Even if you come back to that chat later, it uses the same model and thinking level.
- **To see which model answered**, click More (더보기) (the three dots) below the answer and select See response details (응답 세부정보 보기).

If you hit your usage limit on a paid plan, the chat continues with Flash-Lite.[^models] If answers suddenly get shorter, check See response details.

Before handing off a hard task, you can ask like this to judge whether it is worth raising the thinking level.

```
I am about to ask you to find items we can cut from our team's 3-month budget sheet.
First tell me whether this requires thinking through multiple steps or can be answered simply.
If it takes multiple steps, just list the steps in the order you would check them.
```

## Temporary Chat — Conversations That Leave No History

Click Temporary chat (임시 채팅) at the top right to start a **conversation that does not stay in your history**.[^screen][^start]
Temporary chats do not appear in your sidebar chat list or in Gemini Apps Activity (Gemini 앱 활동) (your saved chat history).[^start]

![Screen with "Temporary chat (임시 채팅)" turned on. In the center below "Just stopping by? (잠깐 들르신 건가요?)" is the notice (②): "Temporary chats won't appear in your recent chats and won't be used to improve Google AI. For safety, they are stored for 72 hours." At the top right, the temporary chat icon has changed into a close "×" button (①)](../../../guide/12-gemini/assets/shot-05-temp-marked.png)

When you turn on Temporary chat, ② the notice appears, and the top-right icon changes to ① Close (×). Click × to return to a normal chat.[^screen]

Use Temporary chat in situations like these:

- When you want to ask a question without leaving it in your chat list
- When you want a fresh answer without the influence of past chats or your custom settings

In exchange, there are things you cannot do.[^start]

- **Once you leave a temporary chat, you cannot view that conversation again.** Copy any answer you need before leaving.
- You cannot use **Connected Apps** like Gmail or Drive.
- You cannot get **personalized responses** tailored to your info, and you cannot leave **feedback** (thumbs up or down) on answers.
- It only works on a **personal Google Account**. It is not available on work or school accounts.

**Even temporary chats are kept in your account for 72 hours.** The on-screen notice explains this is "for safety." However, they are not used to train Google's AI models.[^screen][^privacy]
So even in a temporary chat, do not enter sensitive information like passwords or government ID numbers ([Stage 1 Chapter 9](../00-common/09-safety.md)). We cover detailed privacy settings in [Chapter 9](09-privacy.md).

Two items are worth checking once when you start:

- **Theme (테마)**: By default, it follows your device's light or dark setting. You can switch to a dark screen here, and the choice is saved in that browser.[^theme]
- **Activity (활동)**: Clicking this opens Gemini Apps Activity (Gemini 앱 활동) in a new tab. This is where you decide how your chat history is saved. We cover it in detail in [Chapter 9](09-privacy.md#turning-keep-activity-on-and-off).[^screen]

**There is no separate menu for choosing the screen language.** On the web, Gemini follows your browser or device language.[^language]
Regardless of the screen language, if you ask in Korean or English, it replies in that language.[^language]

## Try It Yourself

1. On the home screen, find and click the five places: the sidebar, the text box, the model button, Temporary chat (임시 채팅), and Settings (설정).
2. Send one short question, then use Edit text (텍스트 수정) to add one more condition and get a new answer.
3. Click More (더보기) → See response details (응답 세부정보 보기) below the answer to check which model replied.
4. Turn on Temporary chat (임시 채팅), ask a question, exit, and check that it did not stay in your chat list.
5. Go to Settings (설정) → Theme (테마), switch to the dark theme, and switch it back.

**Continuing project** ([Preparing a Book Club](../00-common/practice-kit.md)): Continuing from the preparation list you got in Chapter 1, ask: "Rearrange this list in date order." If you do not like the answer, edit your question with Edit text (텍스트 수정) and get a new one.

## Summary

- The screen is divided into five areas: the sidebar, the text box, the model button, Temporary chat (임시 채팅), and Settings (설정).
- Open a new chat in the sidebar, and find past conversations using Search for chats (채팅 검색) and the All (전체) list.
- File uploads and creation tools are gathered under "+" in the text box. You can upload up to 10 files at once.
- The models are Flash-Lite, Flash, Pro, and Argon, and the thinking levels are Low, Medium, and High. Higher settings use more of your usage limit. Free accounts cannot choose them manually.
- Temporary chats do not stay in your chat list, but they are kept in your account for 72 hours. Once you leave, you cannot view them again.
- Settings (설정) has three groups: Account, Preferences, and Data & privacy. The screen language follows your browser language.

## Key Terms

| Term | One-line meaning |
|---|---|
| Sidebar | The area on the left side of the screen where your chat list and menus are gathered |
| Model | The type of AI that creates the answer, such as Flash or Pro |
| Thinking Level | How long the AI thinks things through before answering |
| Temporary Chat | A conversation that does not stay in your chat list or activity history |
| Gemini Apps Activity | The place where your Gemini chat history is collected and managed |
