# Chapter 10. Voice and Phone — Asking by Voice, Gemini Live, Mobile and Desktop Apps

> **One-line summary**: If typing feels tedious, ask with the **microphone**. When you want a back-and-forth spoken conversation, use **Gemini Live** on your phone.
> You can also show your camera or screen while you ask. You can call up the desktop app right away with **one keyboard shortcut**.

This guide could not take screenshots of the phone screens in this chapter directly. It was written based on the official help docs, and menu locations may vary by phone.

## Asking by Voice — The Microphone Button

Instead of typing text, you can ask with your voice.

- **Mobile app**: Tap the microphone in the input box and speak. The microphone stays open for **up to 5 minutes** to listen to your question.[^mobilestart]
- **Web**: Click the microphone button ("Voice input (음성기록)") on the right side of the input box. On a Mac, you can also use the shortcut ⌘ + Shift + D.[^screen]

When you ask out loud, the tips you learned in Stage 1 still apply. Say what you want, who it is for, and what shape you want it in ([Stage 1, Chapter 4](../00-common/04-prompt-basics.md)).

Korean is a supported language in the mobile app, and South Korea is a supported country.[^mobile] However, some voice features, like changing the voice, are not available in certain languages.[^voice]

## Gemini Live — Back-and-Forth Voice Conversations

**Gemini Live** is a **back-and-forth voice conversation** where you don't type a word. You can even interrupt while Gemini is speaking to add details or change the topic.[^live]
It works great in situations like these:[^live]

- When you want to **think out loud**, such as brainstorming gift ideas or planning an event
- When you want to **practice out loud for an important moment**, like an interview or a presentation

```
Tomorrow at my neighborhood book club, I have to introduce our group to new members for 1 minute.
I'll try saying it first. Listen to me, and then tell me how to make it shorter and warmer.
```

### Where to Use It and Requirements

The help docs state that you use it in the **Gemini app on an Android phone or tablet**. **You cannot use Live on the web yet.**[^live] You must be 13 or older (varies by country) and signed in.[^live]
The Gemini widget for iPhone (iOS 17 or up) also has a Live shortcut.[^widget] However, since the Live help docs are written for Android, this guide could not verify how much of it works on iPhone.

For now, **you cannot use Gems in Live.**[^live] The tailored feature that uses memories of your past chats in Live is **only available in English in the US**.[^live]

### Starting and Stopping

- **Start**: Tap "Live" at the bottom of the Gemini app, or swipe left. You can also say, "Hey Google, let’s talk Live."[^live]
- **Pause briefly**: Tap "Hold (대기)" to turn off the microphone. Tap "Live" again to resume.[^live]
- **Mute**: Turn off only your microphone while letting Gemini keep speaking.[^live]
- **Continue in text**: Tap "End (종료)" and the same conversation continues as a text chat. You don't need to start a new chat. In that chat, tap "Live" again to return to voice.[^live]
- **Use other apps**: Swipe up from the bottom of the screen to go to another app while keeping Live on.[^live]

Even if you turn off interrupting by voice, you can still tap the screen to stop Gemini. If you turn on captions, all of your Live conversations after that will have captions.[^live]

### Changing Gemini's Voice

You choose a voice in the **mobile app settings**. Tap a name to preview how it sounds, then tap "Confirm (확인)" to save.[^voice] The voice you pick is used not only in Live, but in **every feature that answers out loud**, such as "Listen (듣기)", which reads replies to you.[^voice]

### Showing Your Camera and Screen

If you turn on your **camera** during Live, you can show Gemini what you see and talk about it.[^live] For example, you can point it inside your fridge and ask, "What can I make for dinner with this?"

**Screen sharing** works too. When you do this, **your entire phone screen** is visible to Gemini.[^live] So before you share, check that there are no passwords, payment details, or other people's messages on your screen.

- To stop sharing, swipe down from the top of your screen, then tap "Stop sharing (공유 중지)" on the "Screen Sharing (화면 공유)" card.[^live]
- If you leave the app, the camera turns off, and it won't turn back on automatically when you return. The same goes for screen sharing.[^live]

**Ask for permission first before recording other people or including them in a Live conversation.**[^live] To see how recordings, videos, and screen shares from Live are stored, check [Chapter 9](09-privacy.md#do-humans-read-it-is-it-used-for-ai-training).

## Mobile App — Taking Photos to Ask, Using as Your Assistant

On the mobile app, you can do a few more things than on the web.

- **Take a photo and ask**: Tap the camera in the app, take a photo, and ask questions about that photo.[^mobileuse]
- **Summarize Gmail and Drive**: When Connected Apps are turned on, you can summarize emails and files on your phone too ([Chapter 7](07-skills-apps.md)).[^mobileuse]

### Using It as Your Assistant on Android

On Android, you can set Gemini as your **default assistant** (the helper you call up with a phone button or your voice).[^mobilestart]

- You call it up by pressing and holding the power button or home button. The exact method varies by device.[^mobilestart]
- "Hey Google" isn't available in every language, but even then, you can tap the microphone to talk with your voice.[^mobilestart]
- While looking at another app, you can call up Gemini and **ask about what is on your screen right now**. Screen context ("Screen context (화면 컨텍스트)") must be turned on in your settings.[^mobilestart]

### Using It on the Lock Screen

On Android phones and tablets, you can ask Gemini to do some tasks without unlocking your phone. You turn "Use Gemini without unlocking (잠금 해제하지 않고 Gemini 사용하기)" on or off in settings.[^lock] Even if you allow it, **tasks that need personal information will still ask you to unlock your phone.**[^lock]

If you share your phone with others or often leave it lying around, it is safer to keep this setting turned off.

## Desktop App — Calling It Up Right Away with a Shortcut

How to install the Mac and Windows apps is covered in [Chapter 1](01-getting-started.md#what-is-gemini-and-where-do-you-use-it). Once installed and running, you can call up the app right away with a **keyboard shortcut**.

| Computer | Shortcut | Other features |
|---|---|---|
| Mac | Option + Space | Share window, "Speak to Window (현재 창에서 바로 말하기)" |
| Windows | Alt + Space | — |

Source: Google Help[^mac][^windows]

On the Mac app, you can also do these things:[^mac]

- **Share window**: Go to "Add files and tools (파일 및 도구 추가)" → "Share window (창 공유)" to show Gemini the window you are looking at right now, and get answers tailored to its contents.
- **"Speak to Window (현재 창에서 바로 말하기)"**: Hold down the fn key and speak, then let go to send your question. It hasn't rolled out to everyone yet.

Sharing a window works just like sharing your phone screen. Check first to make sure there is nothing in that window you shouldn't show.

## Try It Yourself

1. In the Gemini app on your phone, tap the microphone and say, "Recommend three dinner menu ideas for tonight."
2. If you use Android, tap "Live" to practice a 1-minute self-introduction, then tap "End (종료)" to see how the same conversation continues in text.
3. In the mobile app settings, preview two or three of Gemini's voices and pick one.
4. If you have the desktop app, call up Gemini with the shortcut (Option + Space on Mac, Alt + Space on Windows).

**Ongoing activity** ([Preparing a Book Club](../00-common/practice-kit.md)): Practice your hosting remarks for the next meeting out loud with Live. When you finish practicing, ask Gemini in the text chat to summarize what you could improve.

## Summary

- Tap the microphone to ask with your voice. The mobile app microphone listens for up to 5 minutes. Korean is a supported language in the app.
- Gemini Live is a back-and-forth voice conversation. Based on the help docs, you use it in the Android app, and it is not on the web yet. Stop with "Hold (대기)" or "End (종료)", and tapping "End (종료)" continues the conversation in text.
- During Live, you can show your camera and screen. Screen sharing shows your entire phone screen.
- You change the voice in the mobile app settings, and it applies to every feature that answers out loud.
- On Android, you can use Gemini as your default assistant and use it on the lock screen. Tasks that need personal information require unlocking your phone.
- Call up the desktop app with Option + Space on Mac, or Alt + Space on Windows.

## Glossary

| Term | One-line meaning |
|---|---|
| Gemini Live | A back-and-forth spoken conversation with Gemini |
| Screen sharing | Showing your device's screen to Gemini |
| Mobile assistant | The default helper you call up with a phone button or your voice |
| Widget | A small window on your home screen that opens app features directly |
| Speak to Window | A Mac feature where you hold the fn key and speak to ask a question |
