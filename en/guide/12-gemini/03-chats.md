# Chapter 3. Managing Chats — Find, Organize, Share, and Export

> **One-line summary**: You can **find, pin, rename, and delete** your past chats from the side menu.
> Use a **public link** to show a chat to others, and **Export** to move a response into a document or email. Just remember that anyone with a public link can read the chat.

The screens shown here are from the web version as of October 2026, and the screenshots show the Korean interface on a paid plan (Google AI Pro). Menu locations may look slightly different on the mobile app.

## Finding Past Chats

Your past chats are listed in the side menu on the left ([Chapter 2](02-screen-tour.md)).[^recent]
When you have many chats, scrolling through the list gets hard. Click Search for chats (채팅 검색, the magnifying glass icon) at the top of the side menu, type a word you are looking for, and press Enter.[^recent][^screen]

For example, if you asked about kimchi ingredients last week, search for "kimchi." A separate search box opens near the top of the screen.[^screen]

**On a personal account, Keep Activity (활동 기록 보관) must be turned on to search, pin, rename, or delete chats.**[^recent]
Keep Activity is the setting that saves your chats to your account. If you are 18 or older, it is turned on by default. You can find it on the Gemini Apps Activity (Gemini 앱 활동) page by clicking Settings (설정) → Activity (활동). How to turn it off and what changes when you do is covered in [Chapter 9](09-privacy.md#turning-keep-activity-on-and-off).[^activity]

On a work or school account, your administrator manages your activity history. In that case, you cannot delete chats yourself.[^recent]

## Pinning, Renaming, and Deleting

**Pin** the chats you open often. Hover your mouse over a chat in the list, and three dots (⋮) appear on the right. Click them to open the chat menu.[^recent][^screen]

Other chat titles were replaced with examples](../../../guide/12-gemini/assets/shot-08-chat-menu-marked.png)

This screenshot was taken with a practice chat, and the other chat titles were replaced with examples. Clicking the three dots (①) opens the menu (②).[^screen]

| Menu | What it does |
|---|---|
| Pin (고정) | Keeps the chat at the top of the list (changes to Unpin (고정 해제) once pinned) |
| Rename (이름 변경) | Changes the chat title |
| Delete (삭제) | Deletes the chat |
| Share conversation (대화 공유) | Creates a public link (see "Sharing a Chat" below) |
| Add to notebook (노트북에 추가) | Saves the chat to a notebook (notebooks are covered in Chapter 6) |

Source: Google Help and live screen[^recent][^screen]

If you already have the chat open, you can do the same things from the three-dot button at the top right (screen reader label: Open menu for conversation actions (대화 작업 메뉴 열기)).[^screen]

![Clicking the three-dot button at the top right of the chat screen (①, Open menu for conversation actions (대화 작업 메뉴 열기)) opens the menu (②) — Share conversation (대화 공유), Pin (고정), Rename (이름 변경), Download PDF (PDF 다운로드), Export to Docs (Docs로 내보내기), Add to notebook (노트북에 추가), Delete (삭제)](../../../guide/12-gemini/assets/shot-09-top-menu-marked.png)

This menu (②) also has Download PDF (PDF 다운로드) and Export to Docs (Docs로 내보내기), which are not in the side menu.[^screen]

Renaming a chat to include **a date or task name**, like "November book club notice," makes it much easier to spot in your list.

**Keep two things in mind before you delete a chat.**[^recent]

- **Deleting a chat also deletes it from Gemini Apps Activity.** It disappears from your activity history as well.
- **Anything you created in that chat is deleted too.** Documents or apps made with Canvas disappear along with it (Canvas is covered in [Chapter 4](04-files-create.md)).

So if you want to keep using a document you made in that chat, move it out with [Export](#exporting-to-other-apps) before deleting the chat.

## Branching a Chat in a New Direction

Sometimes in the middle of a long chat, you want to **try going in a different direction**. For example, while planning a trip, you might want to ask "What if it rains?" separately.

When that happens, click More (옵션 더보기, the three dots) below the response at that point and choose Branch in new chat (새 채팅에서 브랜치 생성).[^screen][^recent] A **new chat** opens, branching off from that exact point. Your original chat stays untouched, so you can come back and continue it later.[^recent]

![The menu opened by clicking More (옵션 더보기) on the toolbar below a response (①: Good response (대답이 마음에 들어요), Bad response (대답이 마음에 들지 않아요), Redo (다시 실행), Copy (복사), More (옵션 더보기)). Red numbers mark ② Branch in new chat (새 채팅에서 브랜치 생성) and ③ Export to Docs (Docs로 내보내기) and Draft in Gmail (Gmail 초안 작성). The menu also includes Listen (듣기), Report legal issue (법적 문제 신고), and Show thinking (사고 단계 표시)](../../../guide/12-gemini/assets/shot-10-answer-menu-marked.png)

Clicking the three dots on the far right of the toolbar below a response (①) opens the menu. Item ② is for branching, and item ③ is used in "Exporting to Other Apps" below.[^screen]

Branching keeps your original chat from getting cluttered with side questions. It is a way to follow the "new topic, new chat" rule from [Stage 1, Chapter 6](../00-common/06-conversation.md) without losing the context from earlier in the conversation.

**The two chats are deleted separately.** Deleting the branched chat leaves the original chat intact, and deleting the original chat leaves the branched chat intact.[^recent]

## Sharing a Chat — Public Links

To show a chat to someone else, create a **public link**. From the chat menu (the three dots in the side menu, or the three dots at the top right), choose Share conversation (대화 공유). You can see it in the chat menu screenshots above.[^screen] A link (`g.co/gemini/share/…`) is created.[^share]

**Anyone with the link can read that chat.** Even people you didn't send it to directly can read it and share it further if they have the link.[^share]
So skim through the whole conversation before sharing. The link shows the **entire chat**. If you uploaded photos, anyone opening the link can view and download them.[^share]

Here is what you should know.[^share]

- **Your name is not attached.** Your name and account are not shown in the URL or on the shared chat page.
- **It shows a snapshot from the moment you created the link.** If you edit or continue the chat afterward, the shared page does not change. To show updated content, create a new link.
- **Anything created in the chat is included.** Canvas documents, images, and videos also appear on the shared page.
- **The person who receives it can continue the conversation.** Anyone who opens the link can click Continue this chat (이 채팅 계속하기) to keep talking in their own Gemini. However, this does not work for chats made with a Gem or for users under 18. Also, **if you created the chat with a paid Google AI plan, the recipient must also have a paid Google AI plan** to continue it.

### Deleting a Shared Link

If you want to stop sharing, go to Settings (설정) → Your public links (내 공개 링크) and delete that link. You can also delete all of them at once with Delete all links (모든 링크 삭제).[^share][^screen]

A few things remain even after you delete the link.[^share]

- **Posts you shared elsewhere stay where they are.** If you pasted the link in an online community or messenger, you need to delete those posts separately on those sites.
- **Your chat itself is not deleted.** Only the link disappears; the conversation stays in your chat list.

On the flip side, **if you delete the chat, its public link disappears too.** A public link only works for as long as the chat remains saved in your activity history.[^share]

Asking a prompt like this before sharing can help you spot personal information you might have missed. Still, don't assume the AI will catch everything—always read through the chat yourself at the end ([Stage 1, Chapter 9](../00-common/09-safety.md)).

```
I want to share this chat with someone else. Please check our conversation so far and list any
sensitive information—like names, phone numbers, addresses, or company names—that I shouldn't show to others.
```

## Exporting to Other Apps

When you want to move a response into a document or email, use **Export**. It creates a new document or email draft right away without copying and pasting.
Click More (옵션 더보기, the three dots) below a response to find Export to Docs (Docs로 내보내기) and Draft in Gmail (Gmail 초안 작성) (marked ③ in the screenshot above).[^screen][^export]

| Menu | Where to find it | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Export to Docs (Docs로 내보내기) | Three dots below a response | Creates a new document in Google Drive |
| Draft in Gmail (Gmail 초안 작성) | Three dots below a response | Creates a new email draft in Gmail |
| Export to Sheets (Sheets로 내보내기) | Three dots below a table | Sends the table inside a response to Google Sheets |

Source: Google Help and live screen[^export][^screen]

Export to Sheets (Sheets로 내보내기) appears when you click the small three dots (…) right below a table. You only see it on responses that contain a table, and tables with images cannot be exported.[^export][^screen]

![Clicking the small three dots right below a table (①) opens the menu (②) — Export to Sheets (Sheets로 내보내기) and Copy (복사). Below that, ③ is the toolbar for the entire response](../../../guide/12-gemini/assets/shot-11-table-menu-marked.png)

The three dots below the table (①) and the three dots below the response (③) open different menus. To export just the table, click ①.[^screen]
Draft in Gmail requires a Gmail account. It does not send the email right away—it saves a **draft**, so you can check the recipient and content before sending it yourself.[^export]

For example, if you get a table using the prompt below and move it with Export to Sheets, you can sort and edit it right away.

```
Make a table of supplies for next month's book club meeting. Use columns for Item, Person in Charge, and Estimated Cost.
Don't use any other apps—just show the table right here in this chat.
```

There is a reason for that second line. When we sent only the first line while writing this guide, Gemini didn't show a table in the chat. Instead, it tried to connect to Google Sheets and got stuck.[^screen] If you want to see the table inside the chat first, state that clearly like in the second line.

Exported documents and emails follow the terms and policies of the service you export them to (Docs or Gmail).[^export]

## Regenerating and Checking Responses

If you don't like a response, **try getting a new one.** Click Redo (다시 실행, the circular arrow) below the response to generate a fresh answer to the same question.[^screen][^regen] You can only do this on the most recent response in a chat.[^regen]
After regenerating, you can click the arrows below the response to flip back and forth and compare versions.[^regen]

**If you know what you want changed, just ask in a follow-up.** You can keep chatting and ask Gemini to change the length, use simpler words, or adjust the tone.[^regen] Redo works best when your question was fine, but the answer just happened to miss the mark ([Stage 1, Chapter 6](../00-common/06-conversation.md)).

| Less helpful | Better request |
|---|---|
| (Clicking Redo over and over) | "Shorten this to three lines" |
| (Clicking Redo over and over) | "Rewrite this in simple words a fifth grader could understand" |
| (Clicking Redo over and over) | "Make the tone more polite" |

Telling Gemini what to change gets you closer to what you want much faster. When you just click Redo, you don't get to choose how the answer changes.

### Checking Sources

If you see a Sources (출처) button at the bottom of a response or inside a sentence, click it. A side panel opens on the right with related links.[^sources]
Those links might be public websites, files you uploaded, or connected Gmail messages and Google Docs.[^sources]
**Not every response includes sources.** If there is no Sources button, that response doesn't have related links attached.[^sources]

Google also clearly states that Gemini can make mistakes.[^accuracy]

- **Don't rely on it as professional advice for medical, legal, or financial matters.**
- **Double-check answers about people in particular.** Inaccurate details can come up.
- **Gemini can even be wrong when explaining how Gemini itself works.** For example, its answers to "How were you trained?" or "How do you cite sources?" may not match how the product actually works. If you are curious about a feature, check this guide or the official help pages.

If you spot an inaccurate answer, click Bad response (대답이 마음에 들지 않아요, thumbs down) below the response and write down what was wrong.[^accuracy] Keep in mind that when you send feedback, that conversation is sent to Google along with it ([Chapter 1](01-getting-started.md#what-to-try-first-when-something-goes-wrong)).

## Try It Yourself

1. Send a short question in a new chat, then rename that chat to "Practice — Today's Date" from the side menu.
2. Pin that chat, and then unpin it.
3. Open Search for chats, search for "Practice," and check that the chat comes up.
4. Use Export to Docs on one response, and check that a new document appears in your Google Drive.
5. Create a public link, then go to Settings → Your public links and delete it.

**Continuing project** ([Preparing a Book Club](../00-common/practice-kit.md)): Ask Gemini to turn the prep list you organized in Chapter 2 into a table, then move it using Export to Sheets. Rename the chat to "Book Club Prep."

## Summary

- Find past chats using the side menu list and Search for chats. On a personal account, Keep Activity must be turned on to search, pin, rename, or delete chats.
- Use a chat's three-dot menu to pin, rename, delete, or share it. Deleting a chat also deletes any Canvas items created in that chat.
- Use Branch in new chat from the three dots below a response to split off mid-conversation while leaving your original chat untouched.
- Anyone with a public link can read the shared chat. It only shows the chat as it was when you created the link. Delete public links in Settings → Your public links.
- Export to Docs or a Gmail draft from the three dots below a response, or to Sheets from the three dots below a table. Gmail exports are saved as drafts only.
- Redo only works on the most recent response. Asking for specific changes in a follow-up is often faster.
- Check supporting links with Sources, and always double-check answers about medical, legal, or financial matters—or about people.

## Glossary

| Term | One-line meaning |
|---|---|
| Pin | Keeping a chat at the very top of your chat list |
| Branch | A new chat split off from the middle of a conversation; labeled Branch in new chat (새 채팅에서 브랜치 생성) on screen |
| Public Link | A web address that lets anyone with the link read a shared chat |
| Export | Moving a response into another app like Docs, Gmail, or Sheets |
| Regenerate | Generating a fresh answer to the same question; labeled Redo (다시 실행) on screen |
| Source | A web page or file link backing up parts of a response |
| Keep Activity | The setting that saves your Gemini chats to your Google account |
