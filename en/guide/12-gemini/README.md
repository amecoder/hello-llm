# Stage 2 · Gemini User Guide

In Stage 1, you learned **how to ask good questions** to any AI. In Stage 2, you will learn **the screen and features of the Gemini service**.
You will see where buttons are, how to organize your chats, and how your information is used.

> **This guide is based on official Google help pages and actual screens checked on October 30, 2026.** Gemini changes its menu names and features often.
> The screenshots show the Korean web screen on a paid plan (Google AI Pro), so some menus may be hidden or locked on a free account.
> If your screen looks different, follow the names shown on your screen. You can check the latest details through the official help links in the footnotes of each chapter.
