# Chapter 11. Hands-On Exercises — Try Gemini's Features Yourself

> **In One Line**: Here are 10 exercises where you **click through the screens and features** of Gemini from Chapters 1–10 with your own hands.
> Each exercise has a "Check Your Work" checklist, so you can verify your progress without anyone grading you.

The screens are based on the web version as of October 2026. Gemini changes menu names and locations often, so if your screen looks different, follow the names shown on your screen.

## How to Do the Exercises

1. **Open Gemini (gemini.google.com) and sign in.** Most exercises use your chat history and settings.
2. **Follow "What to Try" in each exercise.** Copy the prompt in the gray box and send it.
3. **Check off each item under "Check Your Work."** If you miss an item, read the chapter listed under "Where to Review" and try just that part again.

You do not have to do the exercises in order. However, **do Exercise 10 last**. It cleans up the traces of your practice.
"Actions (작업)" in Exercise 8 is only for **Google AI Pro and Ultra**, so if you are on the free plan, just try scheduled chats. Other menus may also be hidden depending on your account, your plan, or whether they have rolled out to you yet. If you do not see a menu, just read through and move on.

Practice on how to ask questions (how to write prompts) is in [Stage 1, Chapter 10: Hands-On Exercises](../00-common/10-practice.md). Here, focus on **buttons and menus**.

You do not need to finish everything at once. Two or three exercises a day is plenty.

### Three Rules to Follow While Practicing

- **Use Temporary chat whenever possible.** A chat opened with "Temporary chat (임시 채팅)" at the top right does not stay in your chat list and is not used to train AI. However, it is kept for 72 hours ([Chapter 2](02-screen-tour.md#temporary-chat-—-conversations-that-leave-no-history)).[^screen][^hub]
  Do the exercises covering history, skills, and schedules (3, 5, 7, and 8) in a regular chat. You cannot reopen or rename a Temporary chat.
- **Make up fake data.** Use **made-up details** instead of real names, contact info, or company documents. You will clean up your practice from regular chats in Exercise 10. See [Stage 1, Chapter 9](../00-common/09-safety.md#what-not-to-put-in) for what you should never enter.
- **Do not click thumbs-up or thumbs-down.** If you send feedback, that chat is used to improve the service even when "Keep Activity (활동 기록 보관)" is turned off ([Chapter 9](09-privacy.md#do-humans-read-it-is-it-used-for-ai-training)).[^hub]

## Exercises

### Exercise 1. Create a Practice Space with Temporary Chat

**When to use this**: When you want to ask a question without leaving it in your history.

**What to Try**: Click "Temporary chat (임시 채팅)" at the top right and send the prompt below. When you get an answer, close it with the × at the top right.

```
I am using Gemini for the first time. Recommend three good questions to practice with today.
```

**Check Your Work**

- ☐ A notice appeared in the center of the screen saying "Temporary chats don't appear in Recent Chats…"[^screen]
- ☐ You copied any answer you wanted to keep before closing (check this off if there was nothing to keep)
- ☐ After closing, this chat is not in the chat list on the side menu

**Where to Review**: [Chapter 2: Screen Tour](02-screen-tour.md)

### Exercise 2. Change the Thinking Level in the Model Button

**When to use this**: When you want a more deeply thought-out answer to a tough question.

**What to Try**: Send the prompt below in a Temporary chat, then raise the thinking level by one step in the model button inside the input box and send the same question again.

```
Explain the difference between saving 300,000 KRW a month for 2 years and putting in 6,000,000 KRW upfront for 2 years,
and how the interest calculation differs, so a middle schooler can understand.
```

**Check Your Work**

- ☐ You clicked the model button to check your current model and thinking level
- ☐ After changing the thinking level, you compared how the length or order of explanation changed
- ☐ You saw that available options vary by plan (if you are on the free plan, just check what is visible and check this off)

**Where to Review**: [Chapter 2: Model Button](02-screen-tour.md#the-model-button-—-model-and-thinking-level)

### Exercise 3. Rename a Chat and Find It Again

**When to use this**: When you need to find a past chat again later.

**What to Try**: Send the prompt below in a regular chat. In the side menu, click the three dots on this chat → "Rename (이름 변경)" and change the title to "[Practice] Book Club Supplies". Then type "Practice" into "Search for chats (채팅 검색)" to find it.

```
Tell me just five things a first-time visitor should bring to a book club meeting.
```

**Check Your Work**

- ☐ The chat title in the side menu changed to "[Practice] Book Club Supplies"
- ☐ You found this chat using "Search for chats"
- ☐ You clicked "Pin (고정)" to stick it to the top of the list, then put it back with "Unpin (고정 해제)"

**Where to Review**: [Chapter 3: Managing Chats](03-chats.md)

### Exercise 4. Upload a Fake Notice and Get It as a Table

**When to use this**: When you want to summarize a long document or change its format.

**What to Try**: Write a fake notice like the one below in a text editor and save it as a file. Upload it using "+" in the input box → "Upload files (파일 업로드)" and send the prompt.

```
November Book Club Notice
Date: Saturday, November 22, 2:00 PM
Place: District Library, 3rd Floor Seminar Room
Book: "Little Women"
Bring: The book, a notebook for notes, 5,000 KRW membership fee
```

```
Pull the date, place, book, and things to bring from the uploaded notice and organize them into a table.
Do not use any other app—just show the table in this chat.
```

**Check Your Work**

- ☐ The date, place, and fee in the table match the notice word for word
- ☐ It did not make up details that are not in the notice (for example, an ending time)
- ☐ You clicked the three dots right below the table and saw "Export to Sheets (Sheets로 내보내기)" (you do not have to click it)

**Where to Review**: [Chapter 4: Files and Creating](04-files-create.md) · [Chapter 3: Exporting to Other Apps](03-chats.md#exporting-to-other-apps)

### Exercise 5. Add Instructions and See How Answers Change

**When to use this**: When you are tired of repeating the same request ("keep it short", "in a table") every time.

**What to Try**: Add the instruction below in "Settings (설정)" → "Personal Intelligence (개인 인텔리전스)" → "Instructions for Gemini (Gemini 요청 사항)". Ask any question in a new regular chat.

```
Put a one-line summary at the very beginning of your answer, and explain hard words in parentheses.
```

**Check Your Work**

- ☐ A one-line summary appeared at the very beginning of the answer in the new chat
- ☐ The instruction you added showed up on the "Instructions for Gemini" screen
- ☐ After practicing, you deleted this instruction or edited it to fit your needs

**Where to Review**: [Chapter 5: Memory and Customization](05-memory.md)

### Exercise 6. Open a Source and Edit a Deep Research Plan

**When to use this**: When you want to check the basis for an answer, or when you need a report gathered from multiple sources.

**What to Try**: First, send the prompt below and open one of the "Sources (출처)" in the answer. Next, turn on "+" → "More tools (도구 더보기)" → "Deep Research", send the same topic, and when the plan appears, click "Edit plan (계획 수정)" to add "Use only official statistics."

```
Tell me how the share of single-person households in South Korea has changed over the past 5 years, with sources.
```

**Check Your Work**

- ☐ You opened a source link and checked whether the numbers in the answer match the original text
- ☐ When the Deep Research plan appeared, you added a condition using "Edit plan"
- ☐ If you did not want to wait for the full report, you stopped it with the stop button (Deep Research uses a lot of your quota)

**Where to Review**: [Chapter 6: Search and Deep Research](06-search.md)

### Exercise 7. Create and Call a Skill

**When to use this**: When you make the same request often.

**What to Try**: Go to "Customization (맞춤설정)" → "Skills (스킬)" → "Create (만들기)" → "Create manually (수동으로 만들기)" and create a skill like the one below. In a new chat, type `/`, pick the skill, and paste fake meeting notes.

```
Name: summarize-meeting-notes
Description: Organizes meeting notes into decisions, action items, and owners. Use when meeting minutes or notes are pasted.
Instructions: When you receive meeting notes, organize them in this order: 1. Decisions made 2. Action items (Task - Owner - Due date) 3. Topics to revisit next time.
If an owner or due date is missing, do not make one up—write "Needs confirmation".
```

**Check Your Work**

- ☐ When you typed `/`, the skill you created appeared in the list (or `@` if it changed to `@`)
- ☐ Tasks without an owner showed "Needs confirmation"
- ☐ After practicing, you cleaned it up via "More (더보기)" → "Deactivate (비활성화)" or "Delete (삭제)"

**Where to Review**: [Chapter 7: Skills and Connected Apps](07-skills-apps.md)

### Exercise 8. Set a Schedule and Delete It from "Scheduled"

**When to use this**: When you want to get the same update every day or every week.

**What to Try**: Send the prompt below in a regular chat. Check if the schedule appears under "Scheduled (예약됨)" in the side menu, then delete it using "More (더보기)". If you are on a paid plan, also click "Actions (작업)" below the input box and send the "Ask me" option. Scheduled chats are rolling out gradually, so this might not work for you yet.

```
Every Monday at 8 AM, summarize this week's weather in Seoul in three lines.
```

**Check Your Work**

- ☐ Gemini replied with a schedule summary (when and how often)
- ☐ You found this schedule in "Scheduled"
- ☐ After practicing, you deleted the schedule

**Where to Review**: [Chapter 8: Delegating Tasks](08-tasks.md)

### Exercise 9. Ask by Voice on Your Phone

**When to use this**: When typing is hard, or when you want to practice speaking out loud.

**What to Try**: Install the Gemini app on your phone and sign in with the same Google account. Tap the microphone and say the prompt below. If you are on Android, try tapping "Live" as well.

```
Tell me three dishes I can make for dinner tonight with the eggs, tofu, and green onions in my fridge.
```

**Check Your Work**

- ☐ What you said turned into text and was sent as a question
- ☐ This chat also showed up in the chat list on your computer browser (if it was a regular chat)
- ☐ If you used Live, you saw the same conversation continue as text after tapping "End (종료)" (check this off if you did not use Live)

**Where to Review**: [Chapter 10: Voice and Mobile](10-voice-mobile.md)

### Exercise 10. Check and Clean Up Your Data Settings

**When to use this**: When you finish practicing, and once every few months.

**What to Try**: Open "Settings (설정)" at the bottom left and check each item on the checklist below.

**Check Your Work**

- ☐ Under "Activity (활동)", "Keep Activity (활동 기록 보관)" is set the way you want (On or Off)
- ☐ You picked an auto-delete period (18 months by default) that suits you
- ☐ Under "My public links (내 공개 링크)", you deleted any links you no longer need (check this off if you never shared a chat)
- ☐ Under "Customization (맞춤설정)" → "Connectors (커넥터)", you clicked "Disconnect (연결 해제)" on any connected apps you do not need
- ☐ You deleted the practice chats created in Exercises 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7. Disconnecting an app or clearing app data still leaves your chat history behind, so delete the chats separately

**Where to Review**: [Chapter 9: Privacy and Safety](09-privacy.md) · [Chapter 3: Managing Chats](03-chats.md) · [Chapter 7: Connected Apps](07-skills-apps.md#connected-apps-—-use-gmail-drive-and-calendar-in-gemini)

## Summary

- You cannot get used to features just by reading about them. Click each button yourself at least once.
- Practice in a Temporary chat whenever possible, and do exercises covering history, skills, and schedules in a regular chat using fake data.
- "Actions" in Exercise 8 requires a paid plan. If other menus are hidden for your account, just read through and move on.
- Check your own progress with the checklist in each exercise, and if you miss anything, read "Where to Review" and try again.
- When you finish practicing, check "Keep Activity", auto-delete, public links, and connected apps, and delete your practice chats.

There are no new terms in this chapter. Terms like Temporary chat, Skills, and Actions are in the "Key Terms" section of their respective chapters.
