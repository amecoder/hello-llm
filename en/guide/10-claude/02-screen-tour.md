# Chapter 2. Exploring the Screen — Where Everything Is

> **One-line summary**: On the Claude screen, you only need to know four places: the **left sidebar** (past chats), the **center message box** (where you ask questions and the "+" button),
> the **model menu below the message box** (model and effort), and **your name at the bottom left** (settings). At first, just using the message box is enough.

The screen shown here is based on the web version as of October 2026, and the screenshots show the free plan in Korean. Claude is currently rolling out a new screen gradually, so yours might look different. If a button name on your screen is different, look for a similar name.

This guide is based on the **new screen**. The account used for the screenshots in this guide has the new screen. Compare the home screen image below with your own screen.

## The Message Box and the + Menu

Type a question in the center message box and press the send button to start a conversation.
Click the **"+" button** at the bottom left of the message box or type **"/"** in the message box to see more features and commands.[^start]

![Menu opened by clicking "+" in the message box. Red numbers mark ① "Upload or add files (파일 업로드 또는 추가)" (Ctrl+U), ② "Skills", ③ "Add connectors (커넥터 추가)", ④ "Design system (디자인 시스템)", ⑤ "Add plugins (플러그인 추가)", and ⑥ "Memory (메모리)" with a checkmark.](../../../guide/10-claude/assets/shot-02-plus-menu-marked.png)

This is the free plan screen. **The red numbers match the numbers in the list below.**[^screen]

- ① "Upload or add files (파일 업로드 또는 추가)": Upload documents or photos from your computer. You can also drag and drop files into the message box ([Chapter 4](04-files-artifacts.md)).[^upload]
- ② "Skills", ③ "Add connectors (커넥터 추가)", ④ "Design system (디자인 시스템)", ⑤ "Add plugins (플러그인 추가)": Menus that attach extra features to Claude. Skills, connectors, and plugins are covered in [Chapter 7](07-projects-tools.md).
- ⑥ "Memory (메모리)": If there is a checkmark, it is turned on ([Chapter 5](05-memory.md)).

At first, knowing just ① is enough.

**"Research"** (리서치), which investigates a topic at length and gives you a report, is also in the "+" menu. You can also type `/deep-research` in the message box ([Chapter 6](06-search.md)).[^new] Because Research is available on paid plans and up, it did not appear in the free account menu above.[^pricing][^screen]

Below the message box, ⑦ "Manual (수동)" is the **permission mode**. It sets how much Claude works on its own. It is visible on free accounts too.[^screen]

!["Permission mode (권한 모드)" menu opened by clicking ⑦ "Manual (수동)". Red numbers mark the descriptions for ① "Manual (수동)" (with a checkmark) and ② "Auto (자동)".](../../../guide/10-claude/assets/shot-04-permission-marked.png)

- ① "Manual (수동)": "Claude asks for confirmation before using a new tool or opening a new site." This is the default for free accounts.[^screen]
- ② "Auto (자동)": "Claude runs on its own and pauses to check with you if something looks unsafe."[^screen]

On Pro and Max, the default is "Auto" (자동).[^new] At first, it is safer to leave it on "Manual". That is because Claude will ask you first before using a new tool or opening a new website. When you give it a task with big consequences, like sending messages, stay close and watch what it does. See [Chapter 8](08-cowork.md#choosing-how-it-asks-—-permission-modes) for details.[^new]

You do not need to memorize every menu. If you are not sure what you can do, just ask Claude directly.

```
Tell me five things I can ask you to do on this screen right now. Also include one example sentence for how to ask for each one.
```

## The Model Menu — Choosing Model, Effort, and Thinking

On the web and desktop app, the current model name and effort level appear **below the message box** (⑥ "Sonnet 5.5 Medium" in the home screen picture). On the mobile app, they appear at the top of the screen.[^start][^screen]
Click this name to open the **model menu**. According to the Help Center, you set three things here.[^model]

| What you set | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Model** | Which Claude you are chatting with |
| **Effort** (노력) | How much care it puts into a single reply |
| **Thinking** (생각) | Whether it goes through a step-by-step reasoning stage before replying |

![Model menu opened by clicking ⑥ "Sonnet 5.5 Medium (Sonnet 5.5 중간)". Red numbers mark ① the model list (Fable 5.1 "Pro or Max (Pro 또는 Max)" · "Upgrade (업그레이드)", Opus 5.5 "Pro" · "Upgrade (업그레이드)", checked Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5), ② "Effort (노력)" Medium (중간), ③ "More models (더 많은 모델)", and ④ the Fable 5.1 note at the bottom.](../../../guide/10-claude/assets/shot-03-model-menu-marked.png)

This is the free plan screen. **The red numbers match the numbers in the list below.**[^screen]

- ① Model list: On a free account, you can only choose Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5. "Upgrade (업그레이드)" appears next to Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5.
- ② "Effort (노력)": Sets how much care Claude puts into each reply (see "Effort" below).
- ③ "More models (더 많은 모델)": View models not shown in the main list.
- ④ Note: "Fable 5.1 is included in the Max plan, or available on Pro with usage credits."

In this free account menu, **a separate Thinking toggle did not appear.** Only ② "Effort" was there.[^screen] The Thinking toggle may appear depending on your plan and model.

You can change these settings at any time during a chat. The change applies **starting with the next reply**.[^model]

### Choosing a Model

Claude has several models of different sizes and personalities. Click the model name to switch, and look under "More models" (더 많은 모델) for models not in the main list.[^model]

Model names change often. Several new models came out in 2026 alone.[^rn] So this guide refers to models by their **role**. Here are the names and availability by plan as of October 26, 2026.

| Role | Name (family) | Free | Pro | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Highest-performing model | Fable | No | Usage credits only | Up to 50% of weekly limit |
| Large model | Opus | No | Yes | Yes |
| Fast model | Sonnet | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Smallest, fastest model | Haiku | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Source: Anthropic pricing page and Help Center[^pricing][^fable][^rn]

**Usage credits** (사용 크레딧) are pay-as-you-go usage separate from your plan's limit. On Pro, the highest-performing model is not included in your plan limit, so it uses these credits from the very start.[^fable]
On Max, "up to 50%" does not mean you get extra limit. It means you **share the same weekly limit** with other models, and you can spend up to half of it on the highest-performing model.[^fable]

Models also get removed from the list over time. Historically, Anthropic has retired most models about a year after release.[^persona] If a new model replaces an old one and its tone feels unfamiliar, show Claude a few past chats you liked and ask it like this.[^persona]

```
Look over a few past conversations that I really liked, and write instructions for yourself that I can give you going forward.
Summarize my preferences for tone, reply length, and format so they come through clearly.
```

The higher the **Effort** (노력), the more thorough the reply. In exchange, it takes longer and reaches your usage limit faster.[^model]

This is the free plan screen (Sonnet 5.5). ① The notice at the top says: "Higher effort means more thorough responses, but takes longer and uses your limit faster."[^screen]
② There are five levels.

| Effort | Good for |
|---|---|
| "Low (낮음)" · "Medium (중간)" | Everyday tasks |
| "High (높음)" | Balance of quality and speed |

Each model marks its recommended effort level. On this screen, it is "Recommended (추천)" next to "Medium (중간)".[^model][^screen] Most of the time, you can leave it as is.

### Thinking — Should It Reason Before Replying?

When you turn on **Thinking** (생각), Claude breaks down the problem, plans an approach, and weighs different paths before replying.[^model] This is also called **extended thinking** (확장 사고).[^start]
Here is how the Help Center says to turn it on or off: click the model name, hover over "Effort", and flip the "Thinking" (사고, or "Extended" (확장)) toggle on or off.[^model]
This toggle may appear depending on your plan and model. It did not appear in the free account menu used for this guide.[^screen] If you do not see the toggle, just read through this section for reference.
Thinking and Effort are **separate settings**. You can use any combination of the two.[^model] On some newer models, Thinking cannot be turned off.[^model]

When Thinking is on, you will see a "Thinking (사고)" indicator counting time while Claude prepares its answer. Click the "Thinking" section above the reply to read a summary of how Claude thought through the problem.[^model]
Reading this summary lets you check what path Claude took to reach its conclusion.
Sometimes Thinking stops midway with a message saying the rest is unavailable. When that happens, try rephrasing your question from a different angle.[^model]

### When Should You Turn It Up?

**For everyday tasks, the default is enough.**[^model] For math calculations, long document analysis, or multi-step problems, try raising Effort or turning on Thinking.[^model]
Whichever setting you choose, **describing your problem specifically** is what matters most ([Stage 1, Chapter 4](../00-common/04-prompt-basics.md#the-5-elements-of-a-good-prompt)).[^model]

Try asking the same question while changing only the Effort level. You will immediately feel the difference between a quick answer and a thorough one.

```
Show me step by step how much an office worker earning 3 million KRW a month needs to save each month to gather 20 million KRW in 3 years, split into two cases: without savings interest and with savings interest.
```

::: details The reply will look roughly like this (example — click to expand)
This is an example created for this guide. Actual replies vary depending on the service and when you ask.

> **1. Without considering interest**
> - 3 years is 36 months.
> - 20,000,000 KRW ÷ 36 months = about 555,556 KRW
> - That is about 18.5% of a 3,000,000 KRW monthly salary.
>
> **2. Considering savings account interest**
> - Let's calculate assuming an annual interest rate of ○%.
> - The money deposited in the first month earns interest for 36 months, the second month's money for 35 months … and the last month's money for 1 month.
> - We find the amount where total principal (monthly deposit × 36) plus total interest (○○ KRW) equals 20,000,000 KRW.
> - Saving about ○○ KRW per month is enough. Because interest is added, it is slightly less than in case 1.
>
> Actual interest rates vary by product, so try recalculating with the rate of the account you plan to open.

**Check if you got a good reply**: Did it split the answer into two cases? Does it show the calculation line by line? Did it state what interest rate (%) it assumed? For case 1, try dividing it once yourself on a calculator.

:::

## Notice That the Model Switched During a Chat

Sometimes you may see a **notice saying "the model switched"**. Several models, including the highest-performing model, automatically run safety checks on questions. If Claude judges a request to fall into a very narrow high-risk area, it hands the question off to another model to answer.[^switch]
Handing off like this is called a **fallback** (passing to a backup model). Targets include offensive hacking techniques or biological risks like viruses and toxins.[^switch] Most questions are unaffected.

Here is what happens when the model switches.[^switch]

- **A notice appears, and the reply is labeled with the name of the model that answered.** Automatic switching is turned on by default.
- **It stays on the switched model for the rest of that conversation.** You can switch it back to the original model yourself. However, if the flagged content remains in the chat, it may switch again. Try editing your earlier message and sending it again.
- **It can also switch because of content you did not type yourself.** The safety check looks at memory, connected apps, web search results, and uploaded files together.

If you do not want automatic switching, go to Settings → "Capabilities (기능)" and turn off "Switch models when a message is flagged (메시지가 플래그 지정되면 모델 전환)".[^screen] If you turn it off, the conversation pauses instead of switching models, and you choose what to do yourself.[^switch]
If your question is harmless and you think it was flagged by mistake, let them know using "Send feedback" (피드백 보내기).[^switch]

## Try It Yourself

1. Compare the home screen picture with your own screen. If they look very different, expand and read the "If you are using the old screen" box.
2. Click the "+" button in the message box, read through the menu items one by one (you do not need to click them), and close it.
3. Click the model name below the message box to check your current model and see which Effort level has "Recommended (추천)" next to it.
4. Send the savings calculation question above once with Effort set to "Low (낮음)" and once with it set to "High (높음)", then compare the replies. If you see a Thinking toggle, click the "Thinking (사고)" box to read the summary too.
5. In Settings → "General", switch "Theme" to Dark, then set it back to whichever you like best.

## Summary

- On the new Claude screen, chat and Cowork are combined into a single conversation, so you do not pick a mode. This guide is based on the new screen (notes on the old screen are in expandable boxes).
- The sidebar holds past chats, and your name at the bottom left holds Settings, Language, "Get help", and "Upgrade plan". You cannot disable the sidebar, only open and close it.
- Use "+" or "/" in the message box to open features like uploading files or Research. "Manual" and "Auto" below the message box set the permission mode.
- Choose the model and effort level from the model menu below the message box (the Thinking toggle appears depending on your plan and model). Free accounts can only pick Sonnet or Haiku. For everyday tasks, the recommended setting is enough.
- Questions judged to be in a high-risk area may be answered by a different model instead. You can tell from the notice and model label, and you can turn this off in Settings → "Capabilities".
- Change the display language under "Language" in the account menu, and change dark mode and fonts in Settings → "General". Settings menu names may vary depending on your screen.

| Cowork | A feature where you hand off multi-step tasks as a whole ([Chapter 8](08-cowork.md)) |
| Sidebar | The vertical menu attached to the left side of the screen |
| Recents | The list of recent chats and tasks |
| Effort | The setting that decides how much care Claude puts into a single reply |
| Extended Thinking | Going through a step-by-step reasoning stage before replying |
| Usage Credits | Pay-as-you-go usage paid for separately from your plan's limit |
| Fallback | Having another model step in and answer a question flagged by safety checks |
| Dyslexic Friendly | A font designed for people who find text difficult to read |
