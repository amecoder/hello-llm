## Who This Guide Is For

- You have tried ChatGPT or Claude, but **only ask one-line questions like a search engine**
- You got weird or generic answers, thought **"AI isn't a big deal,"** and closed it
- Your workplace tells you to use AI, but **you don't know where to start**
- You don't know how to code, but one day **you want to delegate real work to AI**

In this guide, **LLM** (Large Language Model) refers to text-based AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini that answer your questions in writing.
Every technical term is explained in plain words the first time it appears. There is nothing you need to know in advance.

## How You Will Change After Reading

| Right Now | After Reading |
|---|---|
| You ask in one line: "Summarize these meeting notes" | You tell the AI who will read it and what format you want, getting the right answer in one try |
| When you dislike an answer, you start over from scratch | You point out what is missing and why, guiding the AI to fix it in the same chat |
| You paste a long document and use the result as is | You separate data from instructions, and double-check numbers, dates, and sources against the original text |
| You paste text containing personal information as is | You mask names, contact details, and amounts before asking |

## How You Will Learn

1. **Read** — Read Chapters 1 through 9 in order, apply what you learned to your own work in Chapter 10's practice exercises, and master pro techniques in Chapter 11. Click "Mark as read" at the end of each chapter to check it off in your progress bar above.
2. **Quick Quizzes** — Check what you just read with a single question at the end of each section.
3. **Try It Yourself** — Type the exercises at the end of each chapter into a real AI chat window. You can open ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini right from the top menu shortcuts.
4. **Grab and Use** — Copy a prompt that fits your task from the [Ready-to-Use Prompt Library](/en/guide/00-common/prompt-library) and just fill in the blanks.
5. **Check Your Understanding** — Take the 20-question [Understanding Check](/en/guide/00-common/certificate) to earn a Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced badge. This is not an official certification, but a badge to check your own progress.
6. **Aim Higher** — Challenge yourself step by step from Beginner to Expert in the [AI Knowledge Level Test](/en/guide/00-common/level-test), structured like a written certification exam with 5 subjects and 50 questions.
